About the Designer

Aurélia Dublin is a contemporary Irish fashion designer known for blending traditional craftsmanship with modern elegance. Inspired by the wild Atlantic landscapes, ancient Celtic motifs, and the ever‑changing Irish light, her collections tell a story of heritage reborn.

"Heritage is not the past – it's the future, rewoven." — Aurélia Dublin

After graduating from the National College of Art & Design in Dublin, Aurélia worked with luxury houses in London and Paris before launching her own label in 2018. Her debut collection was an instant critical success, praised for its sculptural silhouettes and innovative use of Irish linen and hand‑woven tweeds.

🎓 Education

NCAD Dublin, Central Saint Martins (MA Fashion)

🏆 Awards

Best Irish Designer 2025, European Fashion Award 2024

📍 Studio

Dublin, overlooking the Irish Sea

🏷️ Philosophy

Slow fashion, Irish linen, handcrafted with respect

The brand has been featured internationally in Vogue, Elle, and Harper's Bazaar, and continues to redefine luxury fashion with a unique identity rooted in place and memory. Each garment is crafted with respect for slow fashion principles and a deep connection to Irish artisans. Aurélia lives and works in Dublin, where her studio overlooks the Irish Sea – a constant source of inspiration.
